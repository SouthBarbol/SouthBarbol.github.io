UX Research & Design

The user experience is like plunging into a lucid dream where reality blends with fantasy and the player's pulse beats to the rhythm of the surrounding environment.

Therein lies the difficulty of this field, and it is up to the developer to make the user be immersed in a pleasant, enjoyable and interactive experience that brings them some value.

UI Design

User interface design is the bridge between people and technology: the element that turns complex systems into clear, intuitive and accessible experiences. A well-designed interface should guide the user naturally, reduce friction and make digital services easier to understand and use.

I focus on designing interfaces that respect usability and accessibility principles, combining visual clarity, coherence and functional structure. My goal is to create digital environments that feel simple, organised and meaningful, allowing users to interact with applications, platforms and information systems without feeling overwhelmed by complexity.

Accesibility

I have a good knowledge of the Game Accessibility Guidelines and the WCAG digital standard because of my TFG, which is dedicated to the analysis of accessibility within the video game industry .

Also, in my curricular internship work experience with the company Mental Gaming I participated in the development process of a video game for children with ASD .

Project Management

A digital project is not only about reaching a final delivery, but about guiding ideas, people and technology through a clear and evolving path. Project management helps transform complexity into structure, ensuring that every requirement, task and decision is aligned with the real needs of the project.

Through agile methodologies, continuous feedback and constant follow-up, this field allows teams to adapt, improve and deliver value step by step. I focus on coordinating work, tracking progress, prioritising tasks and encouraging continuous improvement to help projects grow with purpose, efficiency and quality. Good project management turns change into progress.

Rapid Application Development

Technology becomes truly valuable when it transforms complex processes into simple, efficient and reliable experiences. Through visual development platforms and automation tools, it is possible to turn business needs into practical digital solutions with agility and precision.

This field allows me to combine technical thinking, process analysis and user-centred design to create applications, workflows and internal tools that improve efficiency, reduce manual work and support digital transformation within an organisation.

Quality Assurance

I enjoy analysing digital products in depth, understanding how they work, how users interact with them and where their experience can be improved. Since completing my university degree, I have developed a more critical and structured approach to evaluating interfaces, workflows and technical behaviour.

Through my final degree project, I gained experience in identifying usability and accessibility issues, from minor friction points to more significant barriers that can affect the user experience. I am especially interested in applying this analytical mindset to information systems, corporate applications and digital services, supporting testing, issue detection, documentation and continuous improvement.
