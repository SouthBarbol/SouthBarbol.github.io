About me

Hi, I´m Juan Antonio!


I am a junior developer focused on digital solutions and digital transformation, with a background in UX/UI design and digital accessibility.

During my year at the Instituto de Crédito Oficial (ICO) I coordinated the corporate website and intranet on Liferay, followed up a banking application built on a low-code platform and created solutions with Microsoft Power Platform to digitalise business processes.

My degree in Video Game Design and Development gave me a solid base in programming, interface design, QA and teamwork with Scrum and Kanban.

I am aware of digital accessibility standards (WCAG).
Making software accessible should be a universal duty.

My goal is to keep growing in digital transformation, web platforms and UX/UI, building useful, usable and accessible solutions for people.

Services

I combine technical and design skills to turn user needs into useful, usable and accessible digital solutions.
I am always willing to keep learning.

Digital Solutions

I manage web portals and intranets on Liferay, coordinating support, maintenance and continuous improvement.

I build forms and workflow automations with Microsoft Power Apps and Power Automate, and I am certified in low-code development with OutSystems.

UX/UI Design

I research user needs with Design Thinking and turn them into wireframes and prototypes in Figma (Google UX Design Certificate).

I design clear and coherent interfaces following the principles of usability, for web portals, internal apps and video games made in Unity.

Accesibility

I know the WCAG standard and the Game Accessibility Guidelines; my TFG analysed accessibility in the video game industry.

At the ICO I helped identify and follow up web accessibility issues, and at Mental Gaming I worked on a video game for children with ASD.

Project Coordination

At the ICO I coordinated technical teams and suppliers, tracking incidents, requests and tasks in Jira.

I am used to working with Scrum and Kanban, following up requirements and deliveries, and writing technical and functional documentation.

Video Game Development

Graduated in Video Game Design and Development (URJC). I have taken part in team projects such as Chess: Holy War and CyberHell (see below).

I design mechanics and user interfaces in Unity and write structured GDDs.

QA

I perform manual testing and write clear, structured bug reports in Jira, with version control on GitHub.

My TFG gave me experience in detecting usability and accessibility problems, and I would like to keep learning about test automation.
